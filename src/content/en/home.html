01 / Your own base

A second base where life can carry on

A usable base abroad brings several things together: a place you can reach, permission for each family member to stay, somewhere to live and money you can use. School, medical care and work need to fit around that place. We organise those connections with you.

Start with the life you need to protect. A temporary refuge may call for a furnished rental and a return plan. A longer-term second residence or a permanent move may involve a lease, a property purchase and more extensive family arrangements.

Your departure and contingency plan sets out the order of work, who can act for you and what must be ready before you go. We use it to organise the agreed preparation, check the results and keep essential arrangements current. Prepare while you still have time to choose.

03 / How preparation works

Plan. Set up. Maintain. Activate.

  1. 01

    Plan

    We build your family emergency plan around your current arrangements and the situation you want to prepare for. The personal departure and contingency plan connects the destination, documents, money, home and business. You receive a sequence of work, responsibilities, costs to resolve and decisions to make before circumstances become urgent.

  2. 02

    Set up

    We carry out the agreed preparation: obtaining documents, arranging specialist work, organising accommodation or a property purchase, and preparing your arrival. We coordinate appointments, follow up outstanding work and check the handover. Legal, tax and other regulated work is assigned to the appropriate qualified professionals.

  3. 03

    Maintain

    Your base needs attention while you are elsewhere. Through ongoing review and maintenance we track agreed expiry dates, check contacts and accommodation, and update arrangements when your family, work or circumstances change. You know what remains ready and what needs action.

  4. 04

    Activate

    Before you need the plan, we agree who can start it, whom to contact, available support and alternatives if a route or contact fails. Activation and emergency coordination brings the agreed arrangements into use. Its scope, contact hours, costs and delivery capacity are confirmed with you in advance.

04 / The personal reason

Why EXOBASIS exists

For more than 30 years I have been concerned about war, state repression and the loss of personal and economic freedom. I was ostracised, attacked and rejected for my views. Today I live in exile. I have built my own life and feel safe.

I created EXOBASIS for people who want to protect their freedom, their families and what they have worked for. People who see reasons to prepare a way out and want someone to help turn that decision into action.

My aim is to help you establish somewhere life can continue: a place for your family, access to what you need and practical arrangements you understand. Preparation gives you more to rely on when you decide it is time to go.

Raphael RechbergerFounder of EXOBASIS

05 / What you receive

One point of contact. Work that gets done.

Commission a complete departure and contingency plan or the individual work your next step requires. EXOBASIS takes responsibility for organising the agreed assignment.

We connect the decisions that are often handled separately: where your family can stay, how you will pay, who can act for you, what happens to your business and who prepares the home before you arrive.

You have one point of contact to coordinate appointments, obtain missing information, organise suitable specialist work and follow each task through to its agreed result. Existing advisers and completed preparations are included.

What this gives you: a clear direction, completed work you can check, and an accountable contact for the next step.

Personal initial assessment

A paid, prepared 60-minute consultation addresses your main question. We review your information beforehand, discuss the options and explain the dependencies that could change your decision. You receive written priorities and a recommendation for the work to do next, with the agreed follow-up.

You leave with a reasoned decision and a clear next step.

Your personal departure and contingency plan

We connect your destination, family documents, accessible funds, accommodation, business responsibilities and departure arrangements. The plan records what is ready, what must happen first, who is responsible and which decisions or costs need your approval. It becomes the working basis for the preparation you commission.

Planning, practical setup and ongoing support have their own agreed scope and price.

Documents, property and arrival

We organise your document and residence work alongside the place you will use. For a rental or property purchase, we define the location and search brief, arrange searches and viewings, organise independent local legal checks on title and purchase conditions, then coordinate contracts, completion and handover. Keys, utilities, internet and agreed setup are included in the arrival work. We can also arrange inspections of an unoccupied home and preparation before your next stay.

Your proposal separates our work, specialist fees, rental or purchase costs and ongoing care.

Maintain what you have prepared

We review the documents, deadlines, contacts and local arrangements included in your support agreement. That can include property checks, readiness for your next visit, contact tests and changes to family or business responsibilities. You receive the current position and the action needed to keep the preparation usable.

Regular attention keeps yesterday’s preparation useful for your next decision.
